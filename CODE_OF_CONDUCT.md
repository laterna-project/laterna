# Code of conduct

Laterna is a small open-source project. We want its issue tracker, its pull requests and every
other project space to be a place where anyone can take part without being made unwelcome.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith, and say what you mean plainly and kindly.
- Give and take feedback as a way to make the work better.
- Remember that others have less context, another first language, or less time than you.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, and mockery of someone's skills or questions.
- Harassment of any kind: unwelcome attention, sexualized language or images, intimidation,
  following someone across channels after being asked to stop.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks about anyone's age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender
  identity or expression, experience, nationality, appearance, religion or sexual orientation.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.

## Where this applies

In the repositories of the project and anywhere someone speaks for the project.

## Reporting a problem

Write to **laternaserv@proton.me**. Say what happened, where, and include links or screenshots if
you can. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential: we will not share who reported
without their agreement.

## What the maintainers do

Maintainers may remove or edit comments, commits, issues and other contributions that go against
this code, and may ask someone to stop, warn them, or ban them from the project, temporarily or
for good, depending on how serious and how repeated the behavior is.

This text is our own short version of the ideas of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
