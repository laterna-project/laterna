Laterna playback console

A development tool to exercise the server without a real client. The device profile comes from this browser (MediaSource.isTypeSupported); untick a codec to act as a device that cannot play it and force a transcode. ASS subtitles are rendered by JASSUB (libass in WebAssembly), other text subtitles as WebVTT; a subtitle this browser cannot display (PGS) is burned in by the server. Music: untick "flac" to have FLAC files converted to AAC. Books: pages served by the server (CBZ, scanned PDF), EPUB rendered by epub.js, text PDF by the browser. Photos: albums (folders) and the timeline, each photo scaled to the screen width.
